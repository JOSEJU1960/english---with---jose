🇬🇧 English with Jose

Lesson 1 · Meeting someone

A1–A2 · 15 minutos

Meeting someone for the first time

Escucha → comprende → responde → recibe corrección.

Progreso: 0 de 5 pasos

👂 1. Listening

Escucha primero sin mirar el texto.

Ver diálogo escrito

Anna: Hi! My name is Anna. What's your name?

John: Hi, Anna. I'm John. Nice to meet you.

Anna: Nice to meet you too. Where are you from?

John: I'm from Spain. I live in Cartagena.

🧠 2. ¿Qué has entendido?

Where is John from?

🗣️ 3. Your turn

What's your name?

💬 4. Mini conversation

Hi! Nice to meet you. Where are you from?


🔁 5. Repetition

My name is Jose. I'm from Spain.

📚 Vocabulary

  • What's your name? — ¿Cómo te llamas?
  • My name is... — Me llamo...
  • Nice to meet you. — Encantado/a de conocerte.
  • Where are you from? — ¿De dónde eres?
  • I'm from Spain. — Soy de España.
