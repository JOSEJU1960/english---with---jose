🇬🇧 English with Jose

Lesson 2 · At a café ☕

A1–A2 · Aproximadamente 15 minutos

Tu progreso

Progreso: 0 de 5 pasos

1. 🎧 Listening

Escucha esta conversación en una cafetería.

Waiter: Good morning. What would you like?

Jose: I'd like a coffee and a sandwich, please.

Waiter: Would you like milk?

Jose: Yes, please. No sugar.

Waiter: Anything else?

Jose: No, that's all. How much is it?

Waiter: It's six euros.

📚 Vocabulary

coffee — café
sandwich — bocadillo / sándwich
milk — leche
sugar — azúcar
please — por favor
Anything else? — ¿Algo más?
That's all — Eso es todo
How much is it? — ¿Cuánto cuesta?

💡 Grammar

Para pedir algo educadamente usamos:

I'd like + cosa que queremos

I'd like a coffee.
I'd like a sandwich.
I'd like some water.

I'd like = I would like.
